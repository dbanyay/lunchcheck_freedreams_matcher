📋 How to book

Found a hotel on the map? Follow these steps.

  1. Double-check the match: Mistakes can happen during the automatic matching process. Verify the result.
  2. Contact the hotel directly: Ask if they have rooms available for your dates AND confirm you can pay for meals with Lunch-Check.
  3. Get your freedreams voucher: Check your SBB newsletter first! SBB often sends out exclusive partner offers for discounted freedreams vouchers. If you don't have one, visit freedreams.ch.
  4. Book with the hotel on freedreams' site: Use your voucher to secure your stay!
  5. Pay with your Lunch-Check: Use your Lunch-Check card to pay for dinner and breakfast at the hotel.
⚠️ Important: I am not responsible for these results and therefore the bookings. Mistakes can happen during the automatic matching process. Always contact the hotel directly to confirm that you can use Lunch-Check for meals AND that the Freedreams voucher arrangement works at their location before making any purchases.
